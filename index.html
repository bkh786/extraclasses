The Special5 Difference

5 Students. One Teacher. Total Focus.

We enforce a strict cap of 5 students per session — not as a selling point, but as a teaching philosophy. When there are only 5 students, every hesitation is visible. Every question gets answered. Every concept is mastered before the class moves on.

Students per batch — maximum, always, without exception.

No student goes unnoticed

In a batch of 5, the teacher sees every child. If a student hesitates, looks uncertain, or stops participating — the teacher notices immediately and pauses to help.

There is no fear of holding the class back or being judged. Small batches remove the social pressure that stops children from asking the questions they need to ask.

Class Structure

What Happens in Every 90-Minute Session

First
15 Min

Homework & Doubt Clearing

Every class begins with a dedicated session to review school assignments and clear any doubts from the previous class. No child enters a new topic carrying unresolved confusion from the last one.

Next
75 Min

Concept Teaching & Guided Practice

Focused, interactive concept teaching where students learn by doing, not just watching. Teachers explain the "why" behind every concept — not just the "what." Students are asked questions throughout to confirm understanding at every step.

📝

Monthly Written Assessment

A structured test at the end of every month designed to measure genuine concept clarity — not rote recall. Students are tested on application, not just memory.

📊

Detailed Progress Report for Parents

A subject-wise written report is shared with parents every month — covering strengths, areas that need focus, and the teacher's observations. You are never left guessing about your child's progress.

Common Questions

Parents Ask. We Answer.

What subjects and boards do you cover?
We cover all core subjects — Mathematics, Science & EVS, English, Social Studies, Hindi, and Computers — for Class 1 through 10. We are fully aligned to CBSE, ICSE, and all major State Boards.
What happens if my child misses a class?
All sessions are recorded. The recording is shared with the student within 24 hours of the class. Additionally, make-up doubt sessions can be arranged with the teacher on request.
What device and internet connection does my child need?
Any device with a working camera and microphone — laptop, tablet, or smartphone — is sufficient. A stable broadband connection of at least 5 Mbps is recommended. No special software installation is needed.
How much do the classes cost?
Our fees are structured monthly and vary by class group and the number of subjects. Detailed pricing is shared transparently during your free demo consultation — after you've had a chance to experience our classes firsthand.
Can we change the teacher if we are not satisfied?
Yes. If a parent feels the teacher is not the right fit, simply inform us and we will arrange a teacher or batch change without any hassle.
